Standard operating procedures

How cross-office work moves through CollegeDB. The system enforces the guardrails; these are the human steps around them.

SOP 1 — Legal name change at any office (marriage, court order)

Example: an alumni donor visits Advancement after getting married. Any office can take the request; the Registrar owns legal names and must validate documentation before anything changes.

  1. Verify the person's identity and pull up their record (global search — never create a duplicate).
  2. Open Edit bio/contact and enter the new legal name. Save.
  3. The system does not change the name yet. It records the request as a pending change, flags the profile, and automatically opens a verification task in the Registrar's queue with documentation instructions — they are notified instantly.
  4. Tell the person: "The Registrar's office will contact you to collect your marriage certificate or court order — the name updates the moment they validate it."
  5. Registrar: contact the person, collect the documentation, upload it to their record as Legal name-change documentation, then open the task and Approve. The name updates everywhere at once.
  6. The old name, the new name, who requested it, who approved it, and the document all stay on the record permanently (Activity tab) — the paper trail FERPA expects.

Note: a preferred name is not a legal name — it applies immediately like other contact info, with after-the-fact Registrar verification.

SOP 2 — Contact-info fix at the front desk

  1. The person is at your counter; fix the address/phone/email on their record yourself.
  2. The change applies immediately (they leave with it fixed) and the Registrar gets a verification task. If they reject it, the system reverts the value automatically.

SOP 3 — The person needs another office

  1. Don't send them across campus. From their record, click Refer to another office.
  2. Pick the department, describe the need, set a due date. That office's whole team is notified; whoever claims it owns it; the conversation lives on the task, not in email.

SOP 4 — Paper in hand

  1. Scan/upload it to the person's record the moment you receive it, with the right document type.
  2. It routes to the owning office's review queue automatically; verification auto-completes any admissions checklist item waiting on it. Never keep documents in a desk drawer or shared drive.
